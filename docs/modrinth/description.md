Googly Goobers gives every player huge 3D googly eyes, bouncy cartoon eyebrows and a talking mouth. Faces react to everything players do: they talk when people chat or speak, go wild when people scream, and turn dizzy with spinning spiral eyes when someone gets hit.

![Idle, talking, screaming and hit](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/torzzzzzzzz/Googly-Goobers---By-NotTorzz/claude/brave-archimedes-bh5ll9/docs/screenshots/faces_strip.png)

## Googly eyes

Real 3D eyeballs stick out of every player's face: a black plastic rim, a shaded white dome and a big pupil with shiny highlights. The pupils behave like loose googly-eye discs. They follow where the player looks, glance at you, lag behind head turns, drop when landing and bounce off the rim, and the two eyes move independently. Players blink on their own and their eyes bob, pop and bounce as they walk, jump and talk.

## Talking and screaming

When someone talks in chat, their eyes swell to about 1.6 times their size with a springy pop and their mouth flaps wide open in a different shape every syllable, while the eyebrows bounce and the head nods. When someone screams (AAAAHHH, NOOOO, a shouted HELP!!, or yelling into Simple Voice Chat) the eyes grow to about twice their size with tiny terrified pupils, the mouth becomes a huge trembling oval and the head shakes. The face also matches the message: laughing, surprised, shouting, angry, excited, happy or confused. You can make faces on purpose with keybinds too.

With Simple Voice Chat installed, mouths follow the real voice volume of whoever is speaking: louder means wider, and yelling turns into the screaming face.

## Hit reactions

Taking damage from anything turns the eyes into huge black-and-white spirals that spin, the mouth drops open in shock, the head wobbles, the body staggers and cartoon stars orbit the head. A recovery blink brings the normal eyes back. Getting hit always interrupts talking, and the face goes back to talking afterwards if the player is still speaking.

## Settings

Press G to open the menu. Every effect has its own switch, and sliders control eye size, pupil size, mouth size, eyebrow size, eye spacing, face size, how much the eyes grow and the mouth opens when talking, animation speed, overall exaggeration, expression intensity and the hit reaction. Changes apply immediately and are saved for the next time you play. A live preview shows your own skin with nine preview modes (drag to rotate, scroll to zoom), and Player Selection lets you switch googly eyes on or off for individual players.

## Multiplayer

Googly Goobers works on any server for your own screen: you see googly eyes on the players you choose. If the server also has Googly Goobers installed, players who have the mod see each other's looks, talking and expressions are synced, and operators can use /googlygoobers (or /googly) to force googly eyes on or off for players, play hit reactions and check who has the mod. Players without the mod are never affected and always see normal skins.

## Requirements

Fabric Loader 0.20.1 or newer and Fabric API are required. Mod Menu (for a Configure button) and Simple Voice Chat (for voice lip sync) are optional. There is a separate file for each Minecraft version: 26.3 and 26.2 need Java 25, and 1.21.11 needs Java 21.

## Good to know

Googly Goobers is a client-side cosmetic mod. It never changes gameplay and gives no advantage. When, and only when, the server also runs Googly Goobers, your client tells that server whether you show googly eyes and when you chat or make a face, so other players with the mod can see your face animate. Nothing is sent to any other server or service.
